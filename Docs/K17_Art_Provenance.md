# K17 original art

Generated using the integrated image-generation tool with project-owned K16 character art, K13 stone texture and existing market furniture as stylistic references. No Bizarre Brigade sprites, audio, screenshots or extracted textures were incorporated into runtime assets.

Selected character sheets: MarqueeMaw_v2, UmbraCradle_v2, CoinWidow_v2, ChoirEngine_v2, SuitcaseLeviathan_v2, HopperKeg_v2; the other seven new enemies use v1. Every selected character has twelve separate pose rectangles: idle/blink, four walking poses, two anticipation, two release and two recovery drawings. MarketStands has three designs with four drawings each. StageCourier has twelve drawings.

V1 drafts remain as non-referenced source iterations where V2 corrected crowded boundaries. The importer rejects unsafe opaque gutters instead of clipping a limb or relaxing its check. Stable sprite IDs, bottom pivots and platform texture settings follow the existing K16 pipeline.

ClockworkFloor and DreamGardenFloor are two independently generated ground textures. They use the existing multi-layer floor shader, retained grain/patina and material dissolution, not raised map clutter. They are quiet painted materials, not native 4K source images.

New heroes intentionally reuse seven distinct friendly-species portraits/animations; they are not claimed to have seven newly generated character sheets. Five new bosses and eight new enemies have original sheets. Shopkeepers retain their existing authored sheets.
