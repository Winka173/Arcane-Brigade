Tempo, meter, form and sample counts follow the separate scores in `Tools/Audio/Scores/`, superseding the shared motif/form in the historical prompt. See [individual compositions](Toybox_Individual_Compositions.md) and [Foley designs](Toybox_Foley_Designs.md). All non-music contents are replaced; paths, GUIDs, cue parameters and animation lengths are preserved. The tables below describe the original 153-cue integration; additions, routing, provenance and validation are documented in [Toybox_Delivery.md](Toybox_Delivery.md) and the per-file [manifest](Toybox_Asset_Manifest.json).

Generated by `Tools/Audio/gen_contract.py` from manifests, authored weapons and source-verified hook annotations. Applied to the current K13 main branch from the offline AudioHandoff package.

## 1. Integration status

153 cues, 471 SFX/voice/ambience clips and 15 pieces with 60 music stems. W means a source hook exists; R means reserved or intentionally unused for a mechanic the game does not currently have. A library clip alone is not a completed gameplay hook. See section 12 for the generated totals.

Gameplay/economy/roster/scaling are unchanged. New simulation events are appended after BossLanded, never inserted before serialized values. CombatSimulation emits projectile transformations, contact windup, loot drop/attraction, chill application, boss phase and mimic wake; ArenaController and WeaponSandbox consume PlayBatch.

## 2. Event semantics

Windup/Fire use attacker faction. Hit/Kill use victim faction. Fire occurs once per attack or volley, Hit per victim. Cause.RootId identifies the attack; Cause.ItemId distinguishes secondary damage. Proc means relic/item proc, not elemental status. Boss pattern Windup/Fire has no weapon/subject. Large enemies use Radius >= 0.45, not player BodyScale.

PlayBatch routes Fire before Hit so an immediate melee impact does not swallow its swing. Hero melee misses schedule a quiet follow-up after 0.07 s. Audio ordering does not change simulation events, RNG or damage.

## 3. Runtime API and timing

`CheckpointAudio.PlayInterfaceId(id)`, `PlayInterfaceId(id, delay)`, `PlayWorldId(id, position)`, `PlayBatch(events)`, `CancelScheduledPrefix(prefix)`.

Delayed world cues use a clock that freezes with SimulationPaused/focus/background; delayed UI uses unscaled time and remains available during a manual pause. Queue length is capped at 64 and identical near-simultaneous scheduled cues are coalesced. Destroying/disabling an owner clears its queue. Market rebuild/departure cancels obsolete stock cues.

Keeper arrival plays at animation start; its clip lands at 0.98 s. Ally arrival uses up.ally.land at animation start, with an authored impact at 0.405 s; chr.jump is intentionally reserved rather than doubling this sound. Merge gather starts at age 0; merge impact at 0.54 s, star arpeggio 0.07 s later. Stock audio chooses stand.appear for allies and item.pop for relics at arrival delay + 0.55 s. Rarity follows after 0.07 s. Carpet/keeper departures use their actual animation delays.

## 4. Voice and density policy

Eight lanes: danger 0-1, UI 2-3, cosmetics 4-7 (two with Reduced Audio). Per-cue cooldown/voice limits, family cooldowns, attack-root hit deduplication, off-screen culling and cosmetic gate prevent dense armies from producing one sound per projectile. Important market/merge/landing choreography bypasses the global cosmetic gate, but not cue/family limits or pause. Ordinary companion attacks use x0.7 gain.

Simulation pause suppresses non-UI/danger and stops combat tails; focus/background suppresses all. Music ducks smoothly on pause and mutes exactly at zero MusicVolume. Reduced Audio discards low-priority cosmetics and suppresses merchant voice.

Merchant gestures stop the prior voice and defer syllables until the gesture ends; gibberish is word/punctuation-paced, max 14 syllables per line. Hums occur on offer focus, laughs on purchases, gasp on fusion, farewell on departure.

Gain and music settings preserve the supplied audio direction. Technical tests do not certify subjective quality: user approved the previous preview; newly supplied assets and newly wired combinations still need an in-game listening review.

## 7. Attack versus impact

Fire is a single swing/shot/cast cue per attack or volley. Hit uses victim material, critical and secondary-cause mapping; never reuse the fire cue for impact. Split fragment hits use x0.55, bounce/ricochet x0.8. New projectile events represent actual transformations, not per-frame motion. Boomerang return fires only at false -> true Returning.

## 8. Corrected shop semantics

Gold purchases use shop.buy.gold, Eggs/Health use shop.buy.dream after successful persistence. Failed purchases select gold/egg/hp denial; actual save errors use ui.error. Reroll maps legacy 21 to shop.reroll. Hall exit uses shop.descend, dream-to-gold handoff does not play a purchase sound. Altar fusion is emitted by its committed reveal rather than double-playing the merge at transaction time.

## 9. Validation and remaining scope

See `Docs/Audio/Handoff_Integration_Checkpoint.md` and `PreviewExports/AudioHandoff` for actual Unity results. Source-verified W entries are not claims of human auditory approval for every cue or transition.

Reserved clips remain deliberate: redundant jump/land, unused swing variants, enemy.hurt, sig.fish, shatter without a separate mechanic and theme/transition ambience without a theme-routing API. No forced playback is added just to consume every asset.

## 10. Legacy fallback

All legacy numbered cues 0-38 are mapped to library IDs, including reroll 21. Legacy Kenney/procedural clips, four licensed K8 music tracks and procedural merchant syllable remain fallback for missing/corrupt manifests/clips or the library test disable switch. Successful normal imports use the new library for routed events. New explicit ID-only hooks have no meaningful legacy equivalent.

## 11. Asset provenance and future additions

Offline handoff adds ten cues and retimes seven existing cues; original procedural composition, no third-party samples. WAV and OGG use Git LFS. Keep stable Unity metadata. Add future cues in build_sfx.py/sfx_gen.py, generate only changed cue IDs, verify audio and regenerate this contract. Boss projectile patterns currently share boss.attack; dedicated pattern clips, theme music and intro/outro stems are future content, not missing hooks in this delivery.
