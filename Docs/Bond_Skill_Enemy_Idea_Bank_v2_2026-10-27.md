# Idea bank v2: bonds, companion skills, enemy skills (27/10/2026)

Built from all 18 TFT sets (`Docs/Reference/TFT_All_Sets_Traits_And_Abilities.md`). Not a final list: the owner picks. "Wave start" stands for TFT's "combat start". Mechanics were translated for a real-time game where friends follow the hero (no board, no hexes, no bench fights).

Source tags: [S4] = TFT Set 4 and so on.

## A. Bonds (36 ideas, each a different kind of rule)

**Ritual at the start of a wave**
1. **Offering** [S18 Blackthorn, S7 Darkflight, S18 Eldritch]: before a wave, one friend rests on an altar and does not fight; the whole brigade gains its health and part of its stats. At 6 it fights too and survives.
2. **Decree** [S17 Arbiter, S17 Commander]: write a law = cause + effect ("when anyone defeats an elite: the brigade is 3 s faster"). More steps unlock more causes and effects.
3. **Constellation** [S17 Stargazer]: one constellation per run picks which floor patches glow; standing on a glowing patch gives its blessing.
4. **Weather** [S8 Forecaster]: the weather changes every 20 s (rain, sun, wind, fog); each bond and friend likes one weather.

**Objects and places**
5. **Campfire** [S9 Freljord, S10 Disco]: a camp the brigade can gather around; friends in it regenerate and fill their skill bar faster.
6. **Garden** [S18 Elderwood, S15 Rosemother]: seed pods grow into plants each wave; front plants become roots that hold enemies, back plants become thorn-shooters; at 6 they level up.
7. **Spotlight** [S6 Socialite, S10 K/DA]: a moving beam of light on the floor; whoever stands in it gets a bonus, and the hero chooses to lead or to dodge.
8. **Workbench** [S9 Technogenius, S9 Piltover]: a turret that accepts relics as upgrade slots and acts on what is slotted.
9. **Void Egg** [S9 Void]: place an egg; after 8 s it hatches into something that knocks enemies up and fights for you.
10. **Prototype** [S8 Gadgeteen]: every wave a random weapon appears that breaks at the end of the wave; whoever picks it up uses it.
11. **Ice Soldiers** [S12 Frost]: the first enemies to die each wave rise as allied ice soldiers.
12. **Jade Statues** [S7 Jade]: statues you move; friends near one are healed at the wave start.

**Counters fed by events**
13. **Coven (Essence)** [S18 Coven]: Essence from kills, more from lost health; at 40 convert to a reward or keep gathering for a bigger one.
14. **Gambit** [S15 Crystal Gambit, S10 Heartsteel]: Gem Power from kills and from losing; every 3 waves cash out or **Double Down** (double from losses, half from kills).
15. **Souls** [S9 Shadow Isles, S16]: every fallen unit (either side) gives Souls; skills of the bond scale with Souls.
16. **Records** [S7 Whispers, S11 Artist]: every new enemy species seen is recorded (a pinned note); complete sets pay out.
17. **Underdog** [S11 Fortune, S12 Ravenous]: Luck rises the more health you are missing; at very low health a lucky cascade (crits, drops).
18. **Hearts** [S10 Heartsteel]: Hearts from defeating bosses; after N waves a heart converts to max health for the whole run.

**Choice per run**
19. **Mode** [S17 Gun Goddess, S10 Mixmaster]: at the first step choose a mode that rewrites the bond (Conduit, Challenger or Replicator).
20. **Rider** [S18 Sprykin]: one big beast; drag a friend onto it to choose how it fights.
21. **Mutation** [S9 Void mutations, S6 Zaun]: each friend of the bond gets one chosen mutation, once.
22. **Stance** [S15 Stance Master]: duelist, executioner or juggernaut stance for the strongest friend, changeable between waves.

**Transformation and growth**
23. **Mecha** [S17 Mecha, S8 Mecha:PRIME]: friends merge into a mech that takes two places in the roster; when it falls they split apart.
24. **Ascension** [S18 Solar, S9 Shurima]: friends that are 3-star (or have survived N waves) ascend to the next form during the wave.
25. **Sprouting** [S4 Elderwood]: every 2 s all friends of the bond grow a little (stacking up to 5 times), then reset.
26. **Hatching** [S7 Dragonmancer, S4 Tormented]: after N waves a friend transforms into an enhanced form and keeps it.

**Rules of combat**
27. **Eclipse** [S18 Eclipse]: after 8 s of a wave the weakest enemy is defeated every 3.5 s; the hero takes more damage in that window.
28. **Doom** [S17 Doomer]: at the wave start every enemy is marked; the first hit on each steals 12% of its strength and gives it to your strongest friend.
29. **Echo** [S17 Replicator, S9 Multicaster]: every skill of the bond fires a second time, weaker (and at the last step in another place).
30. **Redirect** [S5 Sentinel/Bodyguard, S18 Vanguard]: when the hero would be hit, a friend of the bond may take the hit; the more it takes the louder the retaliation.
31. **Revenant** [S5 Revenant, S2 Soulbound]: the first friend to fall each wave goes to the "Spirit Realm" (untargetable) and returns when the wave is half over.
32. **Thunderclap** [S7 Tempest, S11 Dragonlord, S9 Freljord]: after N seconds into every wave a storm strikes the whole arena once.
33. **Dawn** [S5 Dawnbringer, S4 Divine, S15 Luchador]: once per wave at half health friends heal, cleanse and leap back in, stunning those around.
34. **Hermit** [S1 Ninja, S4 Ninja, S15 Mentor]: only active with exactly 1 or exactly 4 different friends of the bond; powerful but forces a lopsided brigade.
35. **Mirage** [S7 Mirage, S6 Mutant]: its bonus is a different one every run, chosen at random at the start.
36. **Fed** [S6/S16 Glutton]: duplicate copies of a friend can be fed to a friend of the bond for permanent stats instead of merging.

## D. Run-wide mechanic (TFT: one mechanic outside the units each set)
- **Wisps** [S18]: buyable one-wave powers in the shop (7 families).
- **Power Up** [S15]: twice per run pick 1 of 3 permanent upgrades for one friend.
- **Patron** [S17 Realm of the Gods]: at waves 3, 6, 9 choose between two patrons; aligned patron gives a boon and shapes the shop.
- **Quest unlocks** [S16]: friends that appear only after a condition (win with 4 bonds, spend N gold...).
