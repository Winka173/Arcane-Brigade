## Design draft — item 27: "The Waiting Room" (permanent upgrades)

Between runs the player returns to the Waiting Room, a hub they walk around in. Every upgrade tree is an object in the room that gets visibly restored as it levels (a dusty lamp that lights, a wardrobe that fills, a postbox that fills with letters).

**Currencies**
- **Lamp Oil** (common): earned every run — per wave survived, per boss, ×difficulty, + bonuses for first-time discoveries. Never lost on death.
- **Keepsakes** (rare): from bosses the first time on each difficulty, the Keeper, secret events, combos discovered. Buy unlocks, not stats.

**The stations**
1. **The Lantern — the traveller** (stats): max HP +5 per rank, move speed, pickup radius, +1 starting heart-pickup value, crit chance. Small numbers, many ranks.
2. **The Ledger — money**: starting gold, first reroll each shop free, interest cap +1, sell refund 50% → 70%, chest gold +, one extra shop slot at the last rank.
3. **The Nursery — allies**: start the run with a choice of 1 of 3 one-star allies; "Heirloom family": pick one bond family, its first threshold needs one body fewer; small chance a recruit arrives at 2★; an extra combo hint (item 26).
4. **The Wardrobe — hero mastery**: each hero levels by being played; ranks unlock an alternate personal passive to choose at run start, an alternate starter friend, a gauge that starts 25% full, and cosmetics (lantern colour, trail, idle pose).
5. **The Clock — second chances**: *Second Candle* — once per run, revive at 30% HP (ranks: 40%, then +2 s invulnerability); *Rewind* — once per run, replay a lost wave from its shop; *Late Arrival* — skip the first two easy waves with their gold.
6. **The Postbox — letters from the Thursday visitor** (story + choices): each letter unlocked by milestones; reading it lets you pick a permanent "keepsake relic" to add to the starting relic pool (choose 1 of your unlocked keepsakes at run start). Ties the story to progress.

**Guardrails**
- Total permanent combat power capped around +25% (Lantern + Nursery + Museum combined); most of the meta is *options* (choices at run start, revives, unlocks) rather than raw stats.
- Peaceful stays beatable with zero meta; Beyond the Lamp + pacts needs meta. Bot sweep at meta 0 / half / max to re-cut the difficulty table.
- Every upgrade card: icon, one line, current → next value, cost; no wall of text (items 3/11).
- Save format: new `MetaProgress` block in the profile, versioned, old profiles start at zero with Lamp Oil granted for their BestWave.

Owner to choose: which stations ship first (suggested order: Lantern, Ledger, Clock, Nursery, Wardrobe → then Museum, Night Pact, Postbox, Daily Dream, Bell), and how strong the stat cap should be.

### D11 v2 — environmental elements per map (draft for approval)
Each map has 2–3 living elements that change how you move and fight, telegraphed, never a ground pool or a toy:
- **Waiting Room (w1–5)** — *The Number Board*: every 15 s a number is called and one of four doors flies open, a pack pours out of it — stand by the right door to farm it or flee. *Restless Chairs*: chairs slide to block lanes for a few seconds; enemies trip on them too.
- **Post Office (w6–10)** — *Mail Chute*: letters rain down a lane; catching one (walking through) gives a random small buff for 8 s, enemies that are hit get "stamped" (+damage taken). *Paper Gusts*: a visible wind band pushes shots and light enemies sideways. *Return to Sender*: once per wave a giant parcel lands; break it for gold or let it open into an elite.
- **Lamp Hall (w11–15)** — *Moving Light*: the lamps sweep; inside the light you deal +15% damage, enemies in the dark are faster but drop more coins. *The Snuffer's Round*: a giant hand snuffs one lamp at a time — the dark zone wanders. *Moth Swarms*: moths follow the brightest unit (often the hero), revealing it to enemies.
- **Archive Vault (w16–20)** — *The Clock Chime*: every 20 s the big clock strikes; time slows for enemies for 1.5 s, but on the 12th strike (once per wave) everything speeds up for 3 s. *Shifting Shelves*: shelves glide and reshape corridors each minute. *Ledger Debt*: killing enemies inside the ledger's ink circle on the wall pays double gold but raises the next wave's density a little.
