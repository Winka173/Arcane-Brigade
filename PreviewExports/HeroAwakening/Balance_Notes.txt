Hero kits balance (18/10/2026)

Hero mechanic value (HeroMechanicBalance/KitsFinal): transformation and identity damage per weapon-damage per second,
plus healing/prevented hits and gold. Conditional % passives are no longer counted as mechanic value; they are reported
as passive_bonus_share (share of all damage). Median 0.221 W/s (old catalog median 0.264), 26/93 outside the 0.6x-1.6x band.
Remaining outliers: MirrorKeeper (value is mostly prevented shots), TreasureAcorn/VelvetRegent (small share of team damage),
LoneJelly (W inflated by absorbed memories), CometRunner/HourglassStag (low per-second but kills ratio normal).

Enemies buffed because the kits made the brigade stronger:

  OctoberBalance.EnemyDamageRelief .80 -> .96 (+20% enemy damage)
